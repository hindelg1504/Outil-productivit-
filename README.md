# Ce soir on mange – What's for dinner tonight?

A smart meal planning web app that helps you decide what to cook in seconds, without thinking.

## Features

### 🎯 Quick Meal Suggestions
- Get 3 dinner ideas tailored to your available time (15, 30, 45+ minutes)
- Filter by meal type (vegetarian, fish, meat) or ingredients on hand
- Avoids meals you've eaten in the past 7 days
- Mix of meal types across the week

### 📚 Smart Catalog
- Start with 66 curated meal ideas (no photos needed)
- Rate each meal: "Love it", "Maybe", "Not for us", or "Skip"
- Add new meals in seconds—just a name and tags
- Optional photo upload

### 📝 Daily Reminders
- App asks "What did you have yesterday?" at each open
- Quickly add forgotten meals with one click
- Tracks meal history automatically

### 📅 Weekly Planning
- Plan 5+ dinners at once on Sunday
- Auto-generates shopping list with ingredients
- Toggle meals easily by touching them

### 🛒 Shopping List
- Auto-builds from planned meals
- Check items as you shop
- Copy list to clipboard

### 🤖 AI Integration (Optional)
- Ask Claude for new meal ideas with ingredients you have
- Get recipes and ingredient lists
- Powered by Claude AI (uses your account quota)

## Getting Started

1. **First Time Setup**: Sort through the initial 66 meal ideas (5 minutes)
   - Each meal gets a rating based on your preferences

2. **Daily Use**: 
   - Open the "Tonight" tab
   - Get 3 instant suggestions
   - Select one or ask for alternatives

3. **Plan Ahead**:
   - Use the "Week" tab to fill multiple evenings
   - Generate shopping list with one click

## Configuration

The app stores your preferences on your Claude account:
- Household size (number of people)
- Dietary restrictions (ingredients to avoid)
- Meal ratings and history

## Installation

### As a Web App (Mobile)

**iPhone:**
1. Open link in Safari
2. Tap Share button (arrow in square)
3. Select "Add to Home Screen"
4. Choose a name and tap "Add"

**Android (Chrome/Firefox):**
1. Open link in Chrome
2. Tap menu (3 dots)
3. Select "Install app" or "Add to home screen"
4. Confirm

The app will appear as a native app icon on your home screen.

## Browser Support

Works on any modern browser with local storage support:
- Chrome/Edge
- Safari
- Firefox
- Mobile browsers

## Data Storage

- All preferences and meal history stored on your Claude account
- Data persists across sessions and devices
- No data sent to third parties (except Claude AI for suggestions)

## Notes

- The app cannot send push notifications (web limitation)
- Photo uploads work best when logged into Claude
- AI suggestions require Claude API access on your account
